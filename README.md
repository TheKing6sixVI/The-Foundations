# The Foundations — README

**A fantasy settlement game. Open `the-foundations-0-9-9.html` in any browser — no install, no internet needed. Read this first, then create your character.**

## What is this?

The Old World fell in the Fall, and seven settlements rose on its foundations. You manage them, gather resources, build, trade, and fight your way from **Peasant** to **King of the World** by completing missions (10 per rank). Completing a mission pays a reward to your home settlement (khan and food, scaled by rank; bigger missions pay materials, gear, or even a unique weapon). Some missions have stages (shown as Stage 1/3 and so on). Some missions count only in live mode (the clock button must say Live, not Turns). From Lord onward, missions turn diplomatic: form alliances, ally with and reclaim minor settlements, and make peace with the Obsidian Dominion:

Peasant → Settler → Villager → Townsfolk → Merchant → Knight → Lord → Baron → Viscount → Count → Earl → Marquis → Duke → Grand Duke → Archduke → Prince → King → High King → Emperor → Grand Emperor → Eternal King → King of the World

## Your character

When you start, give your character a **name** and an **age** (16–120) and pick a **race**. Your race is locked for the whole run, until you win, die, or reset. Your race benefits follow you everywhere. Open the **🎒 Character** sheet any time to see what you wear and carry, change your weapon, armor, and trinket, and **train** (+10 max health and +10 max stamina, costing more each time).

- **🏠 Human — Ravenholt:** better prices (sell +2 khan, buy −2 khan) and farms +2 food. Combat: Swords +2 damage, Steel Armor +2 reduction.
- **🌳 Veyari — Elderwood:** +4 wood per gather, hunts +2 food. Combat: Bows +2 damage, Iron Armor +3 reduction (Iron is the lightest armor tier, standing in for light armor — no leather tier exists).
- **🏭 Korr — Ironhold:** +4 iron, +2 stone and copper per gather, and forges turn 5 iron into 4 steel. Combat: Warhammers +3 damage, Steel Armor +3 reduction.
- **🗼 Thalen — The Spires:** scholars produce knowledge each day, +4 copper per gather, knowledge sells for khan. Combat: Daggers +2 damage, Mithril Armor +2 reduction.
- **🐉 Draconians — Emberpeak:** party damage +5 in fights. Combat: Two-handed axes +3 damage, Adamantine Armor +3 reduction.
- **🦎 Vesk — Fangmire:** hunts +4 food. Combat: Spears +3 damage, Copper Armor +2 reduction.
- **🧜 Nerida — Tidehaven:** amphibious merfolk, at home in water and on land; farms +4 food. Combat: Maces +2 damage, Copper Armor +3 reduction.

## What you do

Click a settlement on the map to visit it (this opens a zoomed view with a little picture of the place: your houses placed where you chose to put them, roads between them, forest, crops, and animals around the edges, with scenery themed to each settlement — volcanic Emberpeak, swampy Fangmire, coastal Tidehaven, and so on). When you build a house, you pick where it goes: Center, North, East, South, West, or Riverside.

- **Gather** wood, food, stone, iron, and copper. **Smelt steel** at the forge.
- **Build** houses to grow your people, and **prisons** (Stocks, Hanging Cage, Pillory, Jail) to hold criminals — the crowd can even pelt them with tomatoes.
- **Trade** surplus for khan, **send aid** to neighbors (+5 stability there), or **ask them for aid** (they keep a reserve of 20, so they can say no). **Form alliances** for daily gifts — and each race's alliance unlocks a perk: **Korr** cuts forge costs 20%, **Veyari** allies send 10 food a day instead of 5, **Thalen** improves prices (sell +2, buy −2), **Humans** cut building costs 20%, **Draconians** allies send 10 khan a day instead of 5, **Vesk** allies send 5 iron a day, **Nerida** allies send 5 copper a day. Your own race perk is always active, so ally with the other races to collect theirs.
- **Explore** 51 dungeons across 11 areas. Map icons: 🏠 🌳 🏭 🗼 🐉 🦎 🧜 are settlements, 💎 🏚️ ⛰️ are explorable areas, plus 🌋 the Obsidian Isle once war begins. **Scouting** costs 10 stamina (out of your max stamina) and finds a dungeon about half the time; stamina comes back as time passes and fully when you sleep. Results now show right inside the explore window, along with your party and its gear. Doing things costs stamina: gathering and smelting 5, hunting and searching 10, dungeon chests 5, fight rounds 5 (shrines restore 25, sleep restores fully).
- **Dungeons are runs, not single fights:** enter one and choose your path, door by door — each room and the boss fight move the clock 15 game minutes forward (no waiting), so a normal dungeon takes about two hours and a quarter of game time and a big one about three hours and a quarter — a chest room (loot), a mimic (a chest that fights back, guarding good loot), an undead room (skeletons, zombies, ghouls, wraiths), a goblin ambush, a spider drop, a slime, a bat swarm, or a quiet shrine (+25 health and stamina). After 8 rooms (12 in a big dungeon) you reach the boss. Drops are more common now (35% from normal creatures, 75% from big-dungeon creatures), every creature can drop gear, and every 4th dungeon you clear anywhere pays double loot and a guaranteed drop. You can leave any time and keep what you found. Monsters you wound stay wounded, and creatures you beat can **drop wearable gear**: trinkets that boost gathering and hunting, armor sets, or a weapon.
- **New metals:** search rarely turns up **mithril** and **adamantine**, and every big dungeon's boss hoards some. Both trade like other resources and can be sent as aid.
- **Armor** (crafted at the forge): Iron (−2 damage taken), Copper (−3), Steel (−4), Mithril (−6), Adamantine (−8). Crafting a set while you wear rags puts it straight on.

## War, minor settlements, festivals, and the Grand Wonder

**War:** when you reach Knight, the Obsidian Dominion declares war. Raids strike your settlements on some days — settlements with adventurers on guard repel them, undefended ones lose food, khan, and stability. Open Diplomacy and fight the Obsidian Champion: win 7 champion fights to end the war, earn +500 khan, +100 food, +10 stability, and a unique-weapon roll, then ally with the Dominion in Diplomacy (200 khan + 100 food) for 5 iron a day. Or sail to the Obsidian Isle (revealed on the map when war begins) and storm the Obsidian Citadel: conquering the Dominion ends the war outright (+1000 khan, +200 food, +10 stability, a unique-weapon roll) and makes the Dominion your vassal, sending 10 iron a day.

**Minor settlements:** three small independent hamlets sit on the map with just a house icon — Millbrook, Dunley, and Saltford. Click one to ally (50 khan + 25 food, sends 5 food a day to your home), reclaim it as an outpost after allying (100 khan + 50 wood, sends 10 food + 5 wood a day), or trade (buy 10 food for 15 khan, sell 10 wood for 12 khan).

**Festivals:** every season change starts a festival — donate goods or hunt animals to complete it for rewards to your home. The Autumn festival is the **Harvest Feast** (Thanksgiving is November 27, 2026 — the harvest feast fits food, farming, and hunting), and its reward includes a roll for a unique weapon. Open the Festival from the 🎉 Festival button.

**Grand Wonder:** at Emperor rank, build the Grand Wonder in your home settlement (500 wood, 500 stone, 300 iron, 200 steel, 2000 khan) for +2 to all gathering and 20 food + 20 khan a day.

**Banners:** pick your banner (colors + emblem) in your Character sheet — it shows on settlement zooms and in Diplomacy.

**Unique weapons:** festival wins, the war victory, and the clear-every-dungeon mission can grant unique named weapons, randomized from a pool for your race (2 per race). Draconians can claim the Dragon's Claw Sword. Uniques are equipped in your Character sheet and hit harder than any Peerless weapon (up to 18 damage). They are named items, not a new tier.

**Settlements grow together:** if any settlement qualifies to grow on a new day, every settlement grows by the same amount (up to its own housing cap), even if its own food is short — so populations climb faster than they used to.

## Your people work for you

Assign adults to jobs, one at a time or five at a time. Jobs produce every day:

- **Hunter:** +4 food per day, +2 per hunt
- **Farmer:** +2 food per day, +2 per harvest
- **Miner:** +2 stone, +2 iron, +1 copper per day, +2 per gather
- **Woodcutter:** +6 wood per day, +2 per wood gather
- **Scholar:** 1 khan per day (up to 10); Thalen scholars also produce knowledge
- **Adventurer:** fights in your party and defends the settlement

Children study a trade and take it up when they grow up. Settlements you are not visiting hire, gather, and build on their own — visit one after a while and it will tell you what changed.

**Auto-collect:** once a settlement reaches 100 residents and you reach Lord rank, Manage Settlement offers an Auto-collect toggle. When it is on, every 10 game minutes adds 10 of each resource except mithril and adamantine automatically — no stamina, no task slot. It pauses if the population dips below 100 and resumes when it recovers.

## Research, weapons, and fighting

**Each settlement researches separately, and each rank unlocks 2 new research items fitting that rank (42 in total)** — from basic tools as a Peasant to grand projects as an Eternal King. Better Tools and Better Farming cost less than they used to, so the early research comes sooner.

**Weapons** (crafted at the forge): Club, Dagger, Mace, Spear, Sword, One-handed war axe, Bow and arrows, Two-handed axe, Warhammer. Each one upgrades step by step: basic → **Fine** → **Superior** → **Masterwork** → **Peerless**, the best there is. At the forge, pick how many at once (×1, ×5, ×All) to upgrade weapons or craft armor in bulk, and smelting no longer closes the forge, so you can smelt again and again. In Manage People you choose exactly what each adventurer carries (parties of 5 to 10).

Roaming monsters attack settlements, and scouts can be ambushed. Wounded roaming monsters stay wounded until you finish them or they recover after 7 days. You can flee a fight — but losing one ends your run.

## Time and saving

The clock runs **live** (1 real minute = 10 game minutes) or **turn-based** — tap the clock button to switch. Sleep at night to jump to morning. The game saves itself every 5 minutes and when you close it, so you can continue right where you left off. Reset (or death, or victory) starts you back at race selection.

## Your save

Saves live in your browser on your own computer, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you sign in. With a cloud account, your save is stored in Firebase Firestore under your account only. Old saves from earlier versions keep working — research from a very old save stays in your home settlement only. Saves from before the 21-rank ladder migrate automatically: ranks above Lord shift by 9 (old King becomes the new King), 9 empty mission slots are inserted for the new noble ranks, and completed missions stay done. Buildings from old saves are placed on plots automatically.

## Resource bar and costs

The resource bar now stays pinned to the top of the screen while you scroll, so you can always see what you have when picking builds, research, or forge work. And when you can't afford something, its button now tells you exactly what you're missing — for example, "Need 20 more wood, 10 more stone" — instead of just saying you don't have enough.

## Cloud login (Firebase)

Login is optional. Without it, the game works exactly as before, saving only on this device.

Setup (owner only):
1. Create a free Firebase project at console.firebase.google.com.
2. In Build > Authentication > Sign-in method, enable Email/Password.
3. In Build > Firestore Database, create a database and add this rule, so each player can only read and write their own save:
```
rules_version = '2';
service cloud.firestore {
  match /databases/{database}/documents {
    match /saves/{userId} {
      allow read, write: if request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == userId;
    }
  }
}
```
4. In Project settings, copy the web app config values into FIREBASE_CONFIG near the top of the game script, replacing every PASTE_ placeholder. Until that is done, the Account button shows "not set up yet" and nothing changes.
5. Upload this build to GitHub Pages as usual.

How it works for players:
- Sign in (or create an account) from the ☁️ Account button in the top bar.
- First sign-in with no cloud save uploads the current device save, marked as an early (grandfathered) player.
- If the device and the cloud have different saves, the player picks one to keep. The other save is deleted, not kept as a backup, and Cancel deletes nothing.
- Sync is checked on sign-in and on Sync now. Closing the game uploads the current save for that same signed-in session only; it does not write to the cloud on every 5-minute autosave.
- Leaderboard and multiplayer are not built yet; they are planned for later, when there is budget for them.
