# The Foundations — README

**A fantasy settlement game. Play it from this repo's GitHub Pages link — no install needed. (The file itself is `index.html`; open it in any browser and it works offline too.) Read this first, then create your character.**

## What is this?

The Old World fell in the Fall, and four settlements rose on its foundations. You manage them, gather resources, build, trade, and fight your way from **Peasant** to **King of the World** by completing missions (5 per rank):

Peasant → Settler → Villager → Townsfolk → Merchant → Knight → Lord → King → King of the World

## Your character

When you start, give your character a **name** and an **age** (16–120) and pick a **race**. Your race is locked for the whole run, until you win, die, or reset. Your race benefits follow you everywhere. Open the **🎒 Character** sheet any time to see what you wear and carry, change your weapon, armor, and trinket, and **train** (+10 max health, costing more each time).

- **🏠 Human — Ravenholt:** better prices (sell +2 khan, buy −2 khan) and farms +2 food.
- **🌳 Veyari — Elderwood:** +4 wood per gather, hunts +2 food.
- **🏭 Korr — Ironhold:** +4 iron, +2 stone and copper per gather, and forges turn 5 iron into 4 steel.
- **🗼 Thalen — The Spires:** scholars produce knowledge each day, +4 copper per gather, knowledge sells for khan.

## What you do

Click a settlement on the map to visit it (this opens a zoomed view with a little picture of the place: your houses in the middle, roads between them, forest, crops, and animals around the edges).

- **Gather** wood, food, stone, iron, and copper. **Smelt steel** at the forge.
- **Build** houses to grow your people, and **prisons** (Stocks, Hanging Cage, Pillory, Jail) to hold criminals — the crowd can even pelt them with tomatoes.
- **Trade** surplus for khan, **send aid** to neighbors (+5 stability there), or **ask them for aid** (they keep a reserve of 20, so they can say no). **Form alliances** for daily gifts — and each race's alliance unlocks a perk: **Korr** cuts forge costs 20%, **Veyari** allies send 10 food a day instead of 5, **Thalen** improves prices (sell +2, buy −2), **Humans** cut building costs 20%. Your own race perk is always active, so ally with the other races to collect theirs.
- **Explore** 34 dungeons across 7 areas. Map icons: 🏠 🌳 🏭 🗼 are settlements, 💎 🏚️ ⛰️ are explorable areas. **Scouting** costs 10 stamina (out of 100) and finds a dungeon about half the time; stamina comes back as time passes and fully when you sleep. Results now show right inside the explore window, along with your party and its gear.
- **Dungeons are runs, not single fights:** enter one and choose your path, door by door — a chest room (loot), a mimic (a chest that fights back, guarding good loot), an undead room (skeletons, zombies, ghouls, wraiths), or a quiet shrine (+25 health and stamina). After 3 rooms (5 in a big dungeon) you reach the boss. You can leave any time and keep what you found. Monsters you wound stay wounded, and creatures you beat can **drop wearable gear**: trinkets that boost gathering and hunting, armor sets, or a weapon.
- **New metals:** search rarely turns up **mithril** and **adamantine**, and every big dungeon's boss hoards some. Both trade like other resources and can be sent as aid.
- **Armor** (crafted at the forge): Iron (−2 damage taken), Copper (−3), Steel (−4), Mithril (−6), Adamantine (−8). Crafting a set while you wear rags puts it straight on.

## Your people work for you

Assign adults to jobs, one at a time or five at a time. Jobs produce every day:

- **Hunter:** +4 food per day, +2 per hunt
- **Farmer:** +2 food per day, +2 per harvest
- **Miner:** +2 stone, +2 iron, +1 copper per day, +2 per gather
- **Woodcutter:** +6 wood per day, +2 per wood gather
- **Scholar:** 1 khan per day (up to 10); Thalen scholars also produce knowledge
- **Adventurer:** fights in your party and defends the settlement

Children study a trade and take it up when they grow up. Settlements you are not visiting hire, gather, and build on their own — visit one after a while and it will tell you what changed.

## Research, weapons, and fighting

**Each settlement researches separately:** Better Tools, Farming, Buildings, Weapons, Forges, and Trade Networks.

**Weapons** (crafted at the forge): Club, Dagger, Mace, Spear, Sword, One-handed war axe, Bow and arrows, Two-handed axe, Warhammer. Each one upgrades step by step: basic → **Fine** → **Superior** → **Masterwork** → **Peerless**, the best there is. In Manage People you choose exactly what each adventurer carries (parties of 5 to 10).

Roaming monsters attack settlements, and scouts can be ambushed. Wounded roaming monsters stay wounded until you finish them or they recover after 7 days. You can flee a fight — but losing one ends your run.

## Time and saving

The clock runs **live** (1 real minute = 3 game minutes) or **turn-based** — tap the clock button to switch. Sleep at night to jump to morning. The game saves itself every 30 seconds and when you close it, so you can continue right where you left off. Reset (or death, or victory) starts you back at race selection.

## Your save

Saves live in your browser on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Old saves from earlier versions keep working — research from a very old save stays in your home settlement only.
